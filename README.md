Daryeel Dental Clinic, established in 2020 and operating in Galkaio, offers comprehensive dental care services. However, the clinic currently faces challenges in managing appointments and fee payments efficiently. To address these challenges, this proposal outlines the development of an Online Appointment and  Payment System, streamlining operations and enhancing patient convenience.

Problem Statement
The manual system currently used by Daryeel Dental Clinic for scheduling appointments and managing payments has led to issues such as:
Inefficient patient scheduling, resulting in long wait times and double bookings.
Difficulties in tracking fee payments.
Inconvenience for patients who cannot book or pay remotely.
These challenges undermine operational efficiency and the patient experience.

Main Objective
To develop and implement an Online Appointment and  Payment System that simplifies clinic operations and enhances patient satisfaction.

Spacefic Objective
To develop and implement an Online Appointment and  Payment System that simplifies clinic operations and enhances patient satisfaction.

Significance
The proposed system will:
Enhance operational efficiency at Daryeel Dental Clinic.
Improve patient satisfaction through a seamless booking and payment experience.
Reduce administrative burden, allowing staff to focus on core activities.

Scope
The project focuses on developing a web-based application with the following features:
Patient registration.
Appointment scheduling for various dental services.
Online payment processing via multiple payment gateways.
Administrative dashboard for staff to manage appointments and payments.
Notifications via email  for appointment confirmations and payment receipts.
